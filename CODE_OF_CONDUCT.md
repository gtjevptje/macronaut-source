# Code of conduct

Macronaut is maintained by one person. This is not a large community with
moderators and an appeals process, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest,
so this is short.

## The rule

**Be decent to people.** Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good
faith, has less context than you do, and is spending their own free time here.

Concretely, in issues, pull requests, commits and email:

- Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it. "This breaks when the path
  has a space in it" is useful. "Did you even test this?" is not, and it is the
  same information with a worse delivery.
- Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, and unwanted personal attention
  are not welcome here in any amount.
- Do not post other people's private information.
- "It's just a joke" does not change how it landed.

Disagreement is fine and it is often the point. Technical arguments get people
to better answers. Being unpleasant does not, and "I was right" is not a defence
for how you said it.

## Scope

This applies anywhere the project happens: the repositories, issues, pull
requests, commit messages, and email to the maintainer. It applies to me at
least as much as to anyone else.

## If something goes wrong

Email **gerbenvanpoucke0@gmail.com**. It reaches one person: me. I will read it,
I will not share it further without asking you first, and I will tell you what I
decided to do.

What I can actually do is limited and worth being straight about: edit or delete
comments, close a thread, and block an account from the repositories. That is the
whole toolbox. I cannot follow anyone off this project, and I am not a neutral
arbiter — I am the maintainer, and if the complaint is about me then you are
telling me about myself, which is an awkward position to put you in. If that
happens and you would rather escalate, GitHub's own
[reporting process](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
exists and does not go through me.

I would rather have a small, quiet project than a busy unpleasant one, and I
will pick that trade every time it comes up.
